Product copy

In one sentence

LastBrowser is a Chromium-based desktop browser for Windows that brings web pages, separate workspaces, and an AI assistant together in one interface.

Short description

LastBrowser combines browsing with AI assistance in the context of your work. Separate Spaces organize projects and sign-ins; Sidekick helps with reading, summarizing, and working with content. AI features depend on the provider, access method, and build. Local-First distinguishes local storage from deliberately configured cloud features.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does Local-First mean?

Browser data and work are managed locally. Cloud models and external integrations process submitted data with their providers. Local-First is not a blanket offline guarantee.

Are roadmap items already available?

No. Roadmap items describe development goals; they do not confirm shipped features or platform packages.

Where can I find current releases?

See the download page, changelog, and release archive. State the version and platform when reporting test results.
