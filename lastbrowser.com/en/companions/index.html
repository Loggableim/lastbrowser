Five personalities. Find your fit.

One companion accompanies you in each Space. The personality shapes the tone and appearance of your answers.

Nova Default

Direct & practical

Brings structure to your day, makes the next step clear and keeps answers focused and useful.

“Here are the next three steps. Let's start with the one that saves you the most time.”Style example · Everyday tasks & planning

Orion

Analytical & thorough

Helps you compare, research and weigh your options, with clear reasoning and explicit open questions.

“The evidence supports this option. Two assumptions still need a reliable source.”Style example · Research & decisions

Patch

Technical & precise

For code, troubleshooting and technical questions. Prefers concrete solutions, clear steps and fewer detours.

“The missing check causes this failure. Here is the change and what it does.”Style example · Development & troubleshooting

Spark

Creative & imaginative

Explores alternatives with you, finds new angles and gets ideas, drafts and designs moving.

“Three directions: calm and clear, bold and colourful, or unexpectedly minimal. Let's take one further.”Style example · Ideas & design

Zen

Patient & supportive

Explains ideas in approachable steps and gives you room to learn, pause and ask questions.

“Let's take one step at a time. First the basic idea, then a small example.”Style example · Learning & understanding
