# Draft: Contributor terms (CLA or DCO)

**Status: proposal only. No contributor agreement or DCO policy has been
approved or adopted by this document.**

The repository has not established here that the project has authority to
relicense every contribution, that it operates a CLA bot, or that submitting
or reviewing a pull request constitutes agreement to any additional terms.
Current contributor rights and inbound licensing require review by the
relevant rights holders. See `../license-audit.md`.

## Options for review

The project may consider a Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO), a
Contributor License Agreement (CLA), or another contribution process. The
choice, covered projects, authorized recipient, scope of any license grant,
and contributor assent process remain undecided.

If a CLA is proposed, its final text must identify the authorized receiving
entity and the exact rights requested. It would apply only after approval,
publication of the final terms, and affirmative assent through a verified
process. A pull request alone would not provide that assent.

No CLA bot, GitHub Action, comment command, or other automated assent process
is asserted to exist or be active by this draft. Any such process would need
to be separately selected, configured, and verified before it is described
to contributors as operational.
