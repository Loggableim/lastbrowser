GDPR & Privacy-First

Privacy Policy

Our Local-First Commitment: With LastBrowser, you are the sole owner of your data. We do not track, collect, or monetize your web activities.

1. The Local-First Principle

LastBrowser is built as a Local-First desktop application. Browser profiles containing history, bookmarks, tabs, passwords, cookie jars and saved credentials are stored on your device in %APPDATA%\Lastbrowser. LastBrowser does not operate a central service to synchronize those profiles. External AI requests send the data described in section 3 to your selected provider.

2. Zero Involuntary Telemetry

Our binary contains no Google Analytics SDKs, no tracking pixels, and no device fingerprinting trackers. We do not log IP addresses or browsing timestamps.

3. Artificial Intelligence & Model Privacy

When interacting with Sidekick, you control where data travels:

  • Local Ollama models: Inference runs locally when both the model and Ollama endpoint run on your own computer. A remote Ollama endpoint or cloud model may process requests outside your computer.
  • Ollama Cloud and external AI providers: These connections send your requests and the context supplied for the task, such as selected tab content, to the selected provider. Credentials required for the connection are used to authenticate it. That provider's privacy policy and retention periods apply. A cloud connection is not offline processing.
  • Tasks and goals: Starting an AI task or ongoing goal can result in multiple requests within that task. Data transfers depend on the enabled providers and the tools and content authorized for the task.

4. Data Subject Rights (GDPR)

You can delete locally stored browsing data through LastBrowser settings. Local deletion does not remove data already sent to external AI providers. Access and deletion requests for that data are governed by the relevant provider's privacy policy.
