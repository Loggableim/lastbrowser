Pillar 1 · Intelligence

Nova AI & BYOK Freedom (Bring Your Own Key)

No forced $20/month subscription. Lastbrowser plugs directly into your own credentials. Use free Google Gemini CLI authentication with automatic multi-account round-robin: upon reaching quota thresholds (HTTP 429), queries cycle seamlessly to your next account without interruption.

Alternatively, supply personal API keys for Claude 3.5 Sonnet, OpenAI GPT-4o / o3-mini, or connect completely offline local LLMs via Ollama. Customize assistant personality mentalities (Pragmatic, Inquisitive, Technical, Visionary) and name freely.

Pillar 2 · Multitasking

Isolated Spaces with 100% Session Partitioning

Forget messy profile windows or credential leaks. In Lastbrowser, every Space possesses a distinct, persistent partition key (persist:profile:space).

This means: In Space “Work” you are signed into your corporate Google workspace, in Space “Personal” into your private account, and in Space “Research” completely clean. Zero session spillover, native tab organization, and an integrated Space Hub start page with real-time tab counters.

Pillar 3 · Extensibility

Unified Extension & Skill Hub (Chrome MV3 + MCP)

Two major ecosystems united under one roof (Ctrl+Shift+X): Install Chrome MV3 WebExtensions like uBlock Origin Lite, Dark Reader, or Bitwarden with 1 click or directly via web store URLs.

Simultaneously, Lastbrowser supports the open Model Context Protocol (MCP): Connect external tooling and databases via standardized stdio/sse interfaces—compatible with the Claude Desktop ecosystem and enriched with 48 curated agentic skills.

Pillar 4 · Media & Streaming

Castlabs Widevine DRM for Netflix, Disney+ & More

Lastbrowser integrates the official Castlabs Electron runtime with hardware-accelerated Widevine CDM and Video Multi-Part Protection (VMP) code signing.

Protected media streams on Netflix, Disney+, Spotify, and Amazon Prime Video run natively with hardware acceleration. Utilize the side-by-side Split-View to watch lectures or media on the left while taking notes and generating summaries with Nova AI on the right.

Pillar 5 · Developer & Privacy

Native ConPTY Terminal & 100% Local-First Engine

Developers never need to leave the browser: The embedded Windows ConPTY PowerShell terminal opens with a hotkey (Ctrl+\`) inside the sidebar—complete with ANSI colors, process execution, and interactive TUIs.

100% Local-First: Browsing history, SQLite databases, cookies, and keys reside strictly on your local PC in %APPDATA%\Lastbrowser. Zero involuntary telemetry, zero forced cloud lock-in.
