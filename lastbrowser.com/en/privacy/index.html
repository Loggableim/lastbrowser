GDPR & Privacy-First

Privacy Policy

Our Local-First Commitment: With Lastbrowser, you are the sole owner of your data. We do not track, collect, or monetize your web activities.

1. The Local-First Principle

Lastbrowser is built as a Local-First desktop application. All browsing history, bookmarks, tabs, passwords, cookie jars, and API keys reside exclusively on your local machine in %APPDATA%\Lastbrowser. There are no central servers receiving or storing your activity.

2. Zero Involuntary Telemetry

Our binary contains no Google Analytics SDKs, no tracking pixels, and no device fingerprinting trackers. We do not log IP addresses or browsing timestamps.

3. Artificial Intelligence & Model Privacy

When interacting with Nova AI, you control where data travels:

  • Local Models (Ollama): When using Ollama, no prompt data ever leaves your computer. Inference is 100% offline.
  • AI providers: When connecting external AI services, the terms of the selected provider apply. Depending on your configuration, Lastbrowser can use local providers or external services.

4. Data Subject Rights (GDPR)

Because all data is stored on your device, you have immediate, permanent control: you can clear all cookies, cache, and Spaces at any time via settings.
