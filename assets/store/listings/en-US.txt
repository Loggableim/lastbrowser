LastBrowser — en-US

DESCRIPTION
LastBrowser combines a Chromium browser for Windows with the integrated Nova AI assistant. Organize webpages in vertical tabs and workspaces, and use the assistant alongside your open pages.

Features:
• Vertical tabs and workspaces for your web projects.
• Nova AI assistant for questions, summaries, and working with webpage context you provide.
• Connect with your own credentials to tested and qualified AI provider/model combinations; local Ollama models may be available. Untested beta combinations are hidden by default and, where offered, require explicit opt-in in Settings.
• Integrated terminal and support for browser extensions.
• Locally stored browser profiles and tools for clearing browsing data.
• Chats use a single model by default. Teamwork orchestration is an opt-in beta.

LastBrowser is free for personal and commercial use. External AI services may require their own account and paid services. When you use an external AI provider, your request and the context you provide for the task are sent to that provider. Browser profiles are stored locally on your PC.
