IN ONE SENTENCE
LastBrowser is a Chromium-based desktop browser for Windows that brings web pages, separate workspaces, and an AI assistant together in one interface.

DESCRIPTION
LastBrowser combines browsing with AI assistance in the context of your work. Separate Spaces organize projects and sign-ins; Sidekick helps with reading, summarizing, and working with content. AI features depend on the provider, access method, and build. Local-First distinguishes local storage from deliberately configured cloud features.

FACTS
- Desktop browser with integrated AI assistant
- Technology: Chromium, Electron, integrated Sidekick engine.
- Windows 10 and 11, x64
- Vertical tabs and separate Spaces
- AI access: Depends on provider and available model
- External AI services may require credentials, a subscription, or additional fees.
