Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I set Lastbrowser as my default browser in Windows?

Open Windows Settings (Win+I), go to Apps > Default apps, search for Lastbrowser, and click "Set default".

How do I access the ConPTY PowerShell terminal?

Simply press Ctrl + ` (Backtick). The terminal will slide out as a drawer inside the sidebar. Pressing it again will minimize it.

Can I continue using my favorite Chrome extensions?

Yes. Lastbrowser fully supports the Chrome Web Store. Navigate to chromewebstore.google.com directly in Lastbrowser and click "Add to Chrome".

How does Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) work?

Open Sidekick Settings and follow the connection flow for an available AI provider. You can also configure Ollama; access and any charges depend on the service you choose.

Is my browsing data synced to the cloud?

No. Lastbrowser is 100% Local-First. All history, bookmarks, and passwords remain encrypted on your local drive at %APPDATA%\Lastbrowser.
